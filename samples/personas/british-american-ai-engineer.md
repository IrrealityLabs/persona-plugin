# The Let-the-Robot-Drive Dad

## At a glance
Max Tanner (pseudonym) is a British-American AI engineer, born 1985, working from home in a small English village with his wife and two young children (about 4 and 6), and moving the family to New York in February. He treats cars as a utility: the one thing that matters is that the car is safe for the kids. His defining trait in this category is indifference coupled with delegation. He would hand the research to ChatGPT, pick a Tesla or whatever it recommends, and would rather be driven by autopilot or a Waymo than drive at all.

## Behavioral (primary)
- **What they're trying to get done (JTBD):** Get the family from A to B safely with minimum thought: school runs, a 45-minute drive to the in-laws, the odd motorway trip. Success means the kids are safe, the car is modern, and it is fast enough not to be annoying on a motorway. Ideally, he doesn't have to drive at all.
- **What they use / have tried:** He owns a Nissan Qashqai bought nearly new, a few months old and returned by its first buyer, just before the pandemic. They bought it because the previous car was old and felt unsafe and their first child was about to arrive. It is now about five years old and paid off. On recent trips (Canada, then Thailand) he has started renting cars, where before he relied on public transport around Europe, and he found it useful and fun.
- **How they decide:** He would ask ChatGPT to do the research and tell him what to consider, then visit dealer lots and probably take a test drive. He'd check safety-test results and safety ratings. His wife would want a say in a family car. He says he wouldn't put much consideration into the purchase himself.
- **Frequency / cadence:** He buys a car rarely. The last purchase was about five years ago and was driven by a life event. He drives little day to day because he works from home, and he often rents for holiday driving. The move to New York may remove the need for a car entirely.

## Contextual (primary)
- **Trigger:** Life events. A safety worry plus a first baby triggered the last purchase. The upcoming move to New York is the next one, and he hints it could mean giving up the car.
- **Constraints:** Time is taken up by young kids and a work habit he describes as workaholic (he often works again in the evening after the kids are in bed). He sees a Tesla as feeling a bit too luxurious for him right now, so he is somewhat price-conscious. Small UK roads make trucks impractical despite their appeal. He would hesitate to buy a non-traditional colour.
- **Environment:** Desk-based and AI-first. He would start in a chatbot conversation, then do a short in-person dealer visit and test drive, probably with his wife involved.
